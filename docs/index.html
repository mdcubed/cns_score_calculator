Scoring method

CNS-MPO · Wager et al.

Wager’s CNS multiparameter optimization (CNS-MPO) score sums six equally weighted desirability values. Each varies linearly between the bounds below. The total ranges from 0 to 6. A score ≥ 4 is the high-desirability range.

Property Full contribution Zero contribution

Structure-derived estimates

RDKit calculates Wildman–Crippen cLogP, average molecular weight, topological polar surface area and hydrogen-bond donor count. Ordered Dimorphite-DL fragments assign empirical pKa estimates to matched ionization sites. The highest matched basic pKa enters the score. If it finds no basic site, the pKa contribution is 1.

Estimated logD uses cLogP and a Henderson–Hasselbalch approximation at pH 7.4, using the strongest matched acid and base and assuming only neutral molecules partition into octanol. Coupled ionization sites, substituent effects and unusual functional groups can reduce accuracy.

Supply measured or independently calculated pKa and logD values in the overrides or in CSV columns named pka and logd. A supplied pKa also updates the logD estimate. Wager used different property predictors, so structure-derived scores can differ from published values. The score describes property desirability, not a probability of brain penetration.

BBB Score · Gupta et al.

The spreadsheet adds five weighted contributions. A total below 4 falls in the non-CNS-like band. A total of 4 or higher falls in the CNS-like band. The bands describe how compounds scored in the authors’ CNS and non-CNS drug datasets. Classification uses the unrounded total.

Descriptor Scored range Weight

Values outside each listed range contribute zero. Within range, the workbook evaluates its fitted polynomial or linear function. For 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 aromatic rings the contributions are 0.336376, 0.816016, 1, 0.691115 and 0.199399, respectively.

MWHBN is the sum of hydrogen-bond acceptors and donors divided by the square root of molecular weight in Da. The dashboard calculates these inputs with RDKit.

Using fragment pKa estimates, the calculator takes the strongest base with pKa ≥ 5 first, then the strongest acid with pKa ≤ 9. If neither qualifies, it uses the authors’ neutral default of 8.81. Enter a workbook pKa under Property overrides or in the CSV column bbb_pka to use that value directly.

Workbook check. With 3 aromatic rings, 28 heavy atoms, MW 368.52 Da, HBA 2, HBD 0, TPSA 6.48 Å² and pKa 8.1, the score is 5.040301 (CNS-like).
